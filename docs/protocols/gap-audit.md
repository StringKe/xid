# Protocol Gap Audit

This document answers one question: where does XID fall short of what a protocol or a provider requires, and what exactly is missing. It is written for anyone evaluating whether XID covers their use case, and for contributors picking up work.

"Open" sections are known shortfalls. "Closed" sections record shortfalls that have been fixed, kept so that a reader can see what the fix was and where its evidence lives. Every evidence cell is a real repository path.

Support levels and evidence levels L0 through L4 are defined in `README.md`.
